Ocean From Here

In the flooded ruins of New York City, four young runaways have made their home. On a stolen kayak, they live like kings in the empty city... Until the unexpected discovery of a baby girl, abandoned due to a strict one-child policy, changes their fates forever. Braving the flooded ruins and even greater dangers on dry land, the young boys test the limits of their friendship on a quest to find her a home.
